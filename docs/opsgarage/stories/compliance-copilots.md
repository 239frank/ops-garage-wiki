# Story: Compliance Work That Stays Reviewable

Compliance is full of repetitive coordination: asking for evidence, checking whether it supports a requirement, shaping a narrative, and preparing the next meeting. That is exactly where a careful copilot can help.

## Evidence Copilot

The Compliance Evidence Copilot is an offline browser demonstration of an evidence workflow. It guides intake, evidence requests, evidence review, narrative drafting, and meeting packets while keeping the process visible and separated by workstream.

It is not positioned as a free-roaming agent. It is a practical workflow exercise with clear gates, evidence traceability, and human approval before anything becomes a formal deliverable.

The through-line is simple: put the paperwork in order so the people doing the work can make better decisions.

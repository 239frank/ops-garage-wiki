## Read It in Three Passes

1. **Acquisition**: a prospect finds the site or is discovered through targeted research.
2. **Preparation**: calls and forms become a transcript, analysis, ROI view, and draft report.
3. **Decision**: the operator reviews the work before outreach, reports, implementation, or publishing.

The map is intentionally not a claim that every step is hands-free. It is a view of where the automation helps and where human accountability stays in the loop.

# Leadership Notes

Notes on leadership, operations, team development, and technical decision-making.

## Themes

- Building useful systems
- Training and mentoring
- Translating technical problems for decision makers
- Keeping operations simple and repeatable
- Owning outcomes without overcomplicating the process

## Operating Principle

Useful beats fancy. A tool, dashboard, workflow, or process should make the work easier to understand, easier to repeat, or easier to improve.
